# PI-2026-007: Look-Alike Single Site Outage at SITE-003 (Power vs Transport)

## Summary
Operators initially suspected a transport link issue when `SITE-003` raised `CELL_DOWN` and repeated `SITE_UNREACHABLE` alarms with `dl_throughput_mbps = 0.0`. However, checking the earliest alarms revealed `POWER_FAILURE` and `BATTERY_DISCHARGING` on `SITE-003`.

## Root cause and resolution
Topology check confirmed parent `TRN-AGG-01` had no `LINK_DOWN` alarm and sibling sites `SITE-001` and `SITE-002` had normal 80 Mbps throughput. Root cause was `site_power_failure` on `SITE-003` (`RB-RAN-002`), NOT `transport_link_down`.
