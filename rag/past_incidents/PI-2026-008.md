# PI-2026-008: Look-Alike Throughput Drop on SITE-001 (Congestion vs Backhaul)

## Summary
`SITE-001` reported `LOW_THROUGHPUT` with `dl_throughput_mbps` dropping to 26 Mbps. Engineers checked whether microwave backhaul packet loss (`RB-TRN-003`) was responsible, but `PACKET_LOSS_HIGH` was absent and `HIGH_PRB_UTILIZATION` (major) was active with `prb_utilization_pct` at 96%.

## Root cause and resolution
High PRB utilization (~95%) paired with partial throughput drop (~25 Mbps) confirmed `cell_congestion` on `SITE-001` (`RB-RAN-001`) rather than transport packet loss.
