# PI-2026-003: Commercial Grid Outage and Battery Drain at SITE-002

## Summary
`SITE-002` raised a critical `POWER_FAILURE` alarm followed by `BATTERY_DISCHARGING`, `CELL_DOWN`, and two `SITE_UNREACHABLE` alarms. Both `dl_throughput_mbps` and `prb_utilization_pct` fell to 0.0 on `SITE-002`, while sibling sites `SITE-001` and `SITE-003` on `TRN-AGG-01` remained healthy.

## Root cause and resolution
Diagnosed `site_power_failure` at `SITE-002` per runbook `RB-RAN-002`. Because field dispatch is high-risk (`safe_to_automate: false`), created a `pending_approval` action for `dispatch_field_team`. Operator approved portable generator dispatch and site recovered.
