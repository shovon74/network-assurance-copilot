# PI-2026-004: CORE-MME-01 S1AP Registration Storm

## Summary
`CORE-MME-01` triggered `SIGNALING_OVERLOAD` (major), `CPU_HIGH` (major), and four consecutive `S1AP_REJECT_RATE_HIGH` (major) alarms. KPI `signaling_load_pct` on `CORE-MME-01` surged from 41% to 98%.

## Root cause and resolution
Diagnosed `core_signaling_overload` on `CORE-MME-01` caused by hung NAS signaling contexts after an enterprise IoT reconnect wave. Applied runbook `RB-CORE-001` and auto-executed low-risk action `clear_stuck_session`. `signaling_load_pct` returned to 40%.
