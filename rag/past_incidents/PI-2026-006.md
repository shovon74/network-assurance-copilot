# PI-2026-006: TRN-AGG-02 Uplink Down Affecting SITE-004..006

## Summary
Ten `S1_LINK_FAILURE` alarms and three `CELL_UNREACHABLE` alarms appeared on `SITE-004`, `SITE-005`, and `SITE-006` alongside a single critical `LINK_DOWN` alarm on parent switch `TRN-AGG-02`. All three child sites showed `dl_throughput_mbps = 0.0`.

## Root cause and resolution
Although 13 of the 14 alarms in the incident were RAN alarms on `SITE-004..006`, topology correlation showed `TRN-AGG-02` was their shared parent and had `LINK_DOWN`. Root cause was `transport_link_down` on `TRN-AGG-02` (`RB-TRN-001`), resolved via `reroute_traffic`.
