# PI-2026-009: Distractor Microwave Rain Fade on Transport Trunk

## Summary
During a monsoon squall, a secondary backhaul hop raised `PACKET_LOSS_HIGH` and `LATENCY_JITTER_HIGH`. Downlink throughput dipped to 48 Mbps while `prb_utilization_pct` stayed flat at 44% and no `HIGH_PRB_UTILIZATION` or `LINK_DOWN` alarms occurred.

## Root cause and resolution
Because `prb_utilization_pct` was normal (44%) and `LINK_DOWN` was absent, both `RB-RAN-001` and `RB-TRN-001` were ruled out. Applied `RB-TRN-003` after operator approval.
