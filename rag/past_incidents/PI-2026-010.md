# PI-2026-010: Distractor Baseband Daemon Hang on SITE-006

## Summary
`SITE-006` raised `BBU_PROCESS_HANG` and `CELL_DOWN`. Unlike a site power failure (`RB-RAN-002`), there were no `POWER_FAILURE` or `BATTERY_DISCHARGING` alarms and DC telemetry remained at 53.8V.

## Root cause and resolution
Confirmed baseband software deadlock under `RB-RAN-003`. Operator approved medium-risk `restart_service` to warm-restart the eNodeB baseband process.
