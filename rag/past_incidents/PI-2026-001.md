# PI-2026-001: TRN-AGG-01 Uplink Fiber Cut Cascading to SITE-001..003

## Summary
Critical `LINK_DOWN` alarm fired on `TRN-AGG-01`, followed within 40 seconds by `S1_LINK_FAILURE` and `CELL_UNREACHABLE` alarms across `SITE-001`, `SITE-002`, and `SITE-003`. Downlink throughput (`dl_throughput_mbps`) dropped to 0.0 Mbps on all three child sites for 25 minutes.

## Root cause and resolution
Root cause was `transport_link_down` on `TRN-AGG-01` due to a primary fiber trunk break. Followed runbook `RB-TRN-001` and executed automated low-risk action `reroute_traffic` onto the protection ring. Throughput recovered to ~80 Mbps across all three sites within 2 minutes.
