# PI-2026-002: Evening Rush Hour PRB Congestion at SITE-004

## Summary
`SITE-004` raised `HIGH_PRB_UTILIZATION` (minor, then major 3 minutes later) and `LOW_THROUGHPUT` (major). KPI trends showed `prb_utilization_pct` climbing from 46% to 95% while `dl_throughput_mbps` fell from 81 Mbps to 24 Mbps. Neighboring sites `SITE-005` and `SITE-006` behind `TRN-AGG-02` had normal KPIs.

## Root cause and resolution
Confirmed `cell_congestion` on `SITE-004`. Followed runbook `RB-RAN-001` and auto-executed `reroute_traffic` (Mobility Load Balancing offload). PRB utilization dropped to 62% and downlink throughput returned to 78 Mbps.
